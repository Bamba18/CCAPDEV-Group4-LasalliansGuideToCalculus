Limits and continuity

So far, all of our notions of limits have been quite hand-wavy and imprecise. We are basing it off of visual characteristics of their occurence in graphs of functions, rather than on a condition that can be verified mathematically for any arbitrary function. But there is a common thread through all the discontinuities we have just tackled, which allows us to give a precise definition of the limit.
