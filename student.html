ABOUT THE WEBSITE

Calculus is the study of change, and change is hard to picture on a static slide. The Lasallian’s Guide to Calculus gives every topic a graph you can control: move a slider, press a button, and watch the idea move in real time. Teachers can set up classrooms and students can join them with an invite link.
